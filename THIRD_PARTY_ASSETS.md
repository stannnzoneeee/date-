# Third-party assets

## GIFs

The Milk & Mocha characters and animations belong to their respective creators. These third-party assets are not covered by this repository's code license. Two animations were already referenced by the original project. The GIFs are now bundled locally; PNGs are single-frame alternatives for reduced-motion settings.

- `waiting`: [Milk and Mocha waiting for a reply](https://media1.tenor.com/m/UAFrdZCt29YAAAAC/milk-and-mocha-waiting-for-reply.gif), from the original project.
- `celebrating`: [Milk and Mocha enjoying dessert](https://media1.tenor.com/m/2VprHA868S8AAAAC/yummy-desserts-desserts-milk-mocha.gif), from the original project.
- `shy`: [Milkmocha shy](https://media1.tenor.com/m/OiK1tT84-T0AAAAC/milkmocha-shy.gif), via Tenor.
- `crying`: [Milk and Mocha cry](https://tenor.com/view/milk-and-mocha-cry-sad-tears-upset-gif-17520406), via Tenor.
